- claimant differentiation must reach rhetoric, evidence, environment, and consequence
- the work needs a reader-perspective introduction before the first live hub
- the opening hub has exactly three genuine actions
- branches split again and may reconverge without erasing state
- major irreversible choices require consequential hesitation
- graph-driven state and a static Astro build are sound delivery choices
- artifacts need real interpretive work and intentional presentation
- the visual build must feel authored rather than templated
