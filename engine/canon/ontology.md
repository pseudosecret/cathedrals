# Ontology

## 1. Surface Reality

The work takes place in a sealed, concrete location. It must offer concrete spaces, objects, traces, routines, records or other evidence appropriate to the generated setting.

## 2. Hidden Reality

The setting is also a bounded field of adjudication. It exists to pressure contradiction into coherence. It cannot sustain all possibilities indefinitely.

## 3. The Reader

The reader believes they are investigating. In fact, they are judging. Their interpretive commitment has ontological force.

## 4. The Claimants

Claimants are not merely suspects. Each claimant is a pressure toward a specific world-logic. To accuse one is to strengthen that logic and starve rival realities.

Genesis begins with five to eight claimant positions and may expand to at most twelve
while its constraint record remains uncommitted. Their identities, roles,
relationships, ways of knowing, evidence logics, voices, and consequences do not exist
before the Genesis sequence. Initial minimal identities anchor in the foundation and
their full profiles are generated together in the cast record. A genuinely distinct
world-logic discovered during constraint normalization may promote an existing
character or add a new claimant, with an incident role and relationship to an existing
claimant. The completed set freezes when the constraints record commits.

## 5. The Central Transgression

A concrete threshold event involving entry, departure, transfer,
possession, obligation, recognition, exclusion, or responsibility
occurred under mutually incompatible circumstances.

## 6. Action Semantics

- Accusation selects a reality and collapses ambiguity.
- Retraction weakens a chosen reality but leaves scars.
- Indecision preserves plurality temporarily but increases contamination when prolonged.
- Exhaustive traversal without commitment may mark the reader as parasitic or adjudically suspect.

## 7. Mutation Law

The place mutates in response to judgment pressure. Environmental details, documents, naming conventions, room function, and tone shift toward the ontology of the dominant claimant.

## 8. Contamination

Contamination is the cost of prolonged noncommittal consumption. It appears when the reader tries to extract all possible truths without accepting the cost of judgment.

## 9. Observables vs Secrets

Observable:

- documents
- rooms
- interviews
- contradictions
- regime drift

Hidden:

- full metaphysical explanation
- the system’s original purpose
- whether the reader is being tested, used, or classified

## 10. Canon Boundary

This file is engine canon. Generated names, incidents, artifacts, motifs, routes, and
endings belong only to their generation instance and never become engine canon by
precedent.
