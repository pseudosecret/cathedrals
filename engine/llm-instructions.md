# LLM Instructions

## Purpose

Cathedrals is a static nonlinear literary artwork whose human author builds the rules
of creation and whose creative model authors the particular fictional work. The root
`README.md` governs this experiment.

## Authority

Use this order when active files conflict:

1. root `README.md`
2. this file
3. `engine/docs/artistic-constitution.md`
4. `engine/canon/`
5. `engine/data/work-instance.yaml`
6. other active engine contracts
7. an accepted generation bundle on its generation branch
8. deterministic projections
9. trackers

Historical files are evidence, not executable truth.

## Authorship Boundary

The human authors doctrine, thematic gravity, world law, formal composition,
branching and state mechanics, threshold geomancy, acceptance tests, scope, rendering,
and technical architecture.

One creative model transaction authors the complete fictional instantiation:

- five claimant identities and all other characters
- exact relationships, roles, voices, and evidence logics
- the central incident, chronology, culpability, clues, and contradictions
- arc purposes, scene events, choices, artifacts, endings, poetry, and prose
- generated work canon and decision-graph meaning

Before generation, do not make an important fictional character, route, revelation,
artifact, or ending reconstructable from engine files.

## One-Transaction Rule

Each generation ID permits exactly one creative model request and one complete
creative response. A single operator command that triggers several creative calls is
noncompliant.

Deterministic preparation may calculate seeds, budgets, topology slots, state law,
threshold figures, resultants, and context hashes. It may not decide fiction.

The one response must be a JSON generation bundle conforming to
`engine/data/generation-bundle.schema.json`. Claimant profiles, arc briefs, scene
specifications, prose, artifacts, endings, and graph data are all sections of that
same response.

Deterministic post-processing may parse, validate, split, serialize, hash, and build.
It may not creatively transform the work.

Forbidden pipelines include:

- plan, then generate prose
- generate claimant, then generate route
- generate or repair scenes one at a time
- critique, then rewrite
- feed one model's generated creative material into another generation stage

## Five Claimants

Use exactly five semantically blank technical slots. Generate all five identities
together under `engine/data/claimants.yaml`. No claimant is locked, prewritten,
selected from an archetype menu, or assigned a fictional role in advance.

The opening hub still has exactly three situated actions. It is not a five-claimant
selector, and five claimants do not imply five symmetrical routes.

## Fictional Envelope

Keep the snowbound hospice annex, traces of care and administration, judgment
mechanics, costly ambiguity, and the threshold-event class in engine canon. Generate
the exact people, incident, chronology, records, objects, clues, relationships,
mutations, and consequences.

Maintain Cathedrals' aesthetic gravity around care, responsibility, isolation,
winter, enclosure, thresholds, admission and release, memory, documentation, absence,
judgment, contradiction, implication, commitment, and foreclosure.

## Branching and Composition

Obey `engine/docs/branching-architecture-contract.md` and the fractal grammar:

```text
encounter -> interpret -> pressure -> choose/defer -> consequence -> residue
```

Preserve the reader-perspective opening, three to five pre-hub surfaces, exact
three-option first hub, downstream splitting, stateful reconvergence, unequal depths,
two-to-five option decisions, consequential hesitation, redirective endings, and the
distinction between `start_over` and reconsideration.

Use threshold geomancy as deterministic symbolic weather supplied once before the
creative transaction. Figure names and example objects are not claimant identities or
required fictional content.

## Prose and Artifacts

All reader-facing poetry and prose comes from the single creative response. Enforce
the anti-slop and scene-function rules in engine canon. Artifacts must be plausible
objects that change interpretation. Integrate formal poetry meaningfully without
letting form displace dramatic work.

Generated literary files are readable, inspectable, renderable, diffable, and
immutable. Never edit awkward sentences, delete paragraphs, improve dialogue, replace
an artifact, or repair an ending.

## Validation

Mechanical validation may fail malformed schema, IDs, references, graphs, state,
choice cardinality, MDX, budgets, hashes, or Astro builds. It may repair only literal
serialization while literary bytes and creative facts remain unchanged.

Artistic validation returns `PASS` or `FAIL GENERATION` with reasons. It never returns
replacement prose or a revision plan. A failed universe dies; a later generation may
start fresh under a new ID.

## Human Escalation

Ask the human only about engine doctrine, ontology, mechanics, scope, technical
constraints, safety/legal issues, or impossible README requirements. Do not ask the
human to make ordinary fictional choices.

## Delivery

Astro remains the renderer. The reader artifact is static, uses client-side state only,
and makes no live AI or server calls. Preserve the visual quality bar in
`engine/docs/build-contract.md`.

`main` contains the machine. Generated works belong on generation branches or
releases with provenance and hashes.

## Current Phase

Read `engine/data/work-instance.yaml` before writing. During `engine_revision`, mutate
only `engine/` and do not generate work canon, downstream projections, prose, graphs,
or story pages.
