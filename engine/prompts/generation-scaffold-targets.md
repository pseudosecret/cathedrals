# Choose one backward assignment

Choose the supplied successor entry cases that ONE new predecessor scene will
earn. Return selection with targets keyed by destination node ID, each value its
exact entry-case ID, and a concise causal_job explaining what this scene must
establish and what it will need from earlier scenes. Choose one to five destinations.
One is a chapter continuation; several form a branch. Do not invent IDs or facts.

Work backward from the selected requirements. If an ending requires the reader to
have heard five testimonies, its immediate predecessor might deliver the fifth and
require the other four on entry. A premise describing the archive does not prove
that those experiences already occurred. Avoid grouping mutually exclusive choices
as unconditional guarantees of one scene; choose separate predecessors when needed.

Only the supplied frontier and earlier declarations in this batch are available.
Their entry cases are alternatives, not a demand to read every path. Preserve any
targets fixed by the schema. Do not choose the story's opening merely because this
is the first expansion; the opening is discovered last, near Genesis conditions.

Cover the uncovered cases on every required_targets node. Use remaining_earlier_scenes
to leave room for the other pending cases.

When the schema allows selection=null, it finishes only this batch. Use that to
avoid unnecessary scenes; it does not finish the scaffold. The engine/model makes
all choices automatically. No human assignment is required.
